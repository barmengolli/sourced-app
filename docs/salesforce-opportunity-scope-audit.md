# Salesforce Opportunity scope audit

Status: disabled, manual-trigger-only, read-only discovery. This workflow
does not write to Salesforce, Supabase, Google Sheets, Sourced deals, the
Opportunity staging ledger, or the review queue. Its only successful terminal
contains aggregate counts.

## Decision being validated

The proposed initial Opportunity population is:

- Salesforce Opportunity `CreatedDate` in 2025 or 2026;
- current `RecordType.DeveloperName` is `High_Potential_Prospect`, `Leads`,
  or `Licensing` (Sourced HPP, Opportunity, and Pursuit respectively);
- Customer Expansion is exactly `New Logo` or `New Business`;
- both open and closed Opportunities are retained, but only open records
  appear in the Current Pipeline view;
- every eligible record enters source review;
- an Opportunity created by the approved Dave/David Cummins or Garrett
  McNally Salesforce User is classified as BDR-generated and receives a
  `Marketing SDR` suggestion, but still requires human approval;
- Primary Campaign Source is evidence only and never decides attribution;
- missing or unrecognized New Logo/New Business values are excluded and
  reported in aggregate QA.

The audit intentionally excludes 2024. That year may be added later with its
different NA BDR identity as a separate reviewed scope extension.

## Fields

Confirmed fields:

- `Commercial_Region__c` (Commercial Region)
- `GTM_Cube__c` (GTM - Cube)
- `Amount` (primary visible revenue)
- `SaaS_Revenue__c` and `SaaS_Revenue_USD__c` (stored but initially hidden)
- `Existing_Customer_or_New_Business__c` (New Logo/New Business gate)
- `CreatedById` (BDR identity)

The API name for the Salesforce label `Market` is deliberately not guessed.
The workflow reads Opportunity `FieldDefinition`, requires exactly one field
whose label is `Market`, validates its API-name shape, and inserts the
discovered name into the Opportunity SOQL query. Zero or multiple matches fail
the run.

## Manual-overwrite contract for the later production sync

The audit records the approved contract but creates no storage:

- Salesforce evidence refreshes on every successful sync.
- A Sourced manual override wins for Market, Commercial Region, GTM - Cube,
  and source channel.
- A refresh may update the stored Salesforce value but may not clear or
  replace a manual override.
- The UI will expose an explicit “Use Salesforce value” action to remove an
  override intentionally.
- Record Type/current pipeline stage remains Salesforce-controlled.

This requires separate source-value and override-value storage in the later
production implementation. It must not be simulated by simply stopping all
updates to a field after the first import.

## Workflow artifact

`src/generated/salesforceOpportunityScopeAudit.workflow.json`

The artifact is generated by
`scripts/build-salesforce-opportunity-scope-audit-workflow.mjs`. Run
`npm run check:opportunity-scope-workflow` to prove the committed JSON has not
drifted from its generator.

Safety properties:

- `active: false`
- one Manual Trigger; no schedule or webhook
- Salesforce `search` nodes only
- no HTTP, Supabase, Sheets, database, or write-capable node
- no credentials or pinned data
- no Salesforce IDs, Opportunity names, account names, or owners in the
  aggregate terminal
- GUARD is the only successful terminal

The private Opportunity query node necessarily holds source rows in the n8n
execution. Do not share or export that node’s output. Share only
`GUARD: aggregate-only scope audit`.

## First run

1. Import the generated JSON into n8n.
2. Confirm the workflow is inactive.
3. Attach the existing read-only Salesforce OAuth credential to all three
   Salesforce nodes:
   - `READ ONLY: Resolve approved BDR users`
   - `READ ONLY: Describe Opportunity fields`
   - `PRIVATE: Read candidate Opportunities - DO NOT SHARE`
4. Execute the workflow manually.
5. The only successful terminal must be
   `GUARD: aggregate-only scope audit`.
6. Share that aggregate output only.

Review these result groups before authorizing any write workflow:

- eligible and excluded Opportunities;
- exclusions by missing, existing-customer, unrecognized, year, and invalid
  source row;
- 2025 versus 2026 counts;
- current HPP/Opportunity/Pursuit and open/closed totals;
- approved BDR versus other/missing creator;
- source-review suggestions and Primary Campaign Source coverage;
- Market, Commercial Region, GTM - Cube, and revenue-field coverage.

Only after those totals are accepted should the daily staging and review-queue
workflow be designed. Production Opportunity ingestion will follow the shared
Sourced schedule of 11:50 PM `America/Denver`; this audit itself has no
schedule.
