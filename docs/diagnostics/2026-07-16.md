## M1: cost attached to a channel outside the selected year

**Query (logical):**
```sql
select cc.channel_id, ch.year, count(*), sum(cc.amount)
from campaign_costs cc
join channels ch on ch.id = cc.channel_id
group by cc.channel_id, ch.year;
```
**Result:** all 4 `campaign_costs` rows attach to `year = 2026` channels. No cost row references a channel outside 2026.

**Disposition:** M1 is an approved exclusion policy (Section 4.1), not a live repair. There are currently no rows that would be excluded. The policy is protected by an active unit test (Step 2), not by production data.

## M2: parent spend roll-up discarding the parent's own budget

**Query (logical):**
```sql
-- cost rows and whether their channel is a parent, and whether any child has its own cost
select cc.channel_id, ch.name, ch.year, cc.amount, cc.start_date, cc.end_date,
       (select count(*) from channels k where k.parent_channel_id = cc.channel_id) as num_children,
       (select count(*) from channels k
          join campaign_costs kc on kc.channel_id = k.id
        where k.parent_channel_id = cc.channel_id) as children_with_own_cost
from campaign_costs cc
join channels ch on ch.id = cc.channel_id;
```

**Result (aggregate, 4 cost rows):**

| channel (taxonomy label) | year | amount | is parent | num children | children with own cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 - Limra L&A | 2026 | $7,500 | no | 0 | 0 |
| 2026 - Naphia Engage | 2026 | $7,500 | no | 0 | 0 |
| 2026 - Semana del Seguro | 2026 | $10,740 | no | 0 | 0 |
| 2026 - Content Syndication | 2026 | **$60,000** | **yes** | 6 | **0** |

- **M2a** (parent with BOTH own direct cost AND descendant direct cost, the shape that loses money): **NONE in production.**
- **M2b** (parent with direct cost, children, zero child cost): exactly one, **Content Syndication $60,000**.

**Descendant lead counts for the Content Syndication parent** (aggregate `count(*)` per `source_channel_id`, no rows returned):

| child (taxonomy label) | leads |
|---|---|
| Credit Protection | 27 |
| Life & Annuity | 55 |
| Protection | 5 |
| Vitech | 26 |
| Group Benefits | 98 |
| P&C | 66 |
| **parent's own direct leads** | **0** |
| **descendant total** | **277** |

**Computed-output reproduction (full-year 2026, all regions):**

The allocation loop (`computeChannelSpend` step 7) fires because the parent has children, no child has direct cost, and descendant leads > 0. It distributes $60,000 across the 6 children by lead share; the slices sum to exactly $60,000. The step-9 roll-up then overwrites the parent's `allocatedCost` with the sum of the children's slices, which is again $60,000.

| | value |
|---|---|
| parent `allocatedCost` before roll-up | $60,000.00 |
| parent `allocatedCost` after roll-up | $60,000.00 |
| difference | **$0.00** |

**Verdict: the live Content Syndication view does NOT currently hide $60,000. The roll-up is a mathematical no-op for this shape** (children's allocated slices sum back to the parent's cost). Per CLEANUP_PLAN_EXECUTION.md Step 1 acceptance, the claim that the live view hides exactly $60,000 is **withdrawn**.

The M2 code defect is real but latent: it only loses money for the **M2a** shape (a parent with both its own direct cost and a child with direct cost), because step 7 then skips allocation (`anyChildDirect` short-circuit) and step 9 overwrites the parent's retained own cost with the children's smaller sum. That shape does not exist in production today.

**Reframed disposition:** M2 (Step 3) is a **preventative guardrail**, not a repair of a live number. It will be implemented and covered by fixtures that construct the M2a shape synthetically. No production Spend-tab number changes as a result.

## M5b: HPP+ deals with no `lead_id`

**Result:** **all 164 attribution rows are leadless** (`lead_id IS NULL`). This is the norm in the current data model, not evidence of Sales origin: the schema comment on `attributions.lead_id` states "M7 leaves lead_id null. M8 will add an 'associate lead' picker." So `lead_id IS NULL` is true for **every** deal regardless of how it actually originated.

**Corrected classification (audit follow-up, 2026-07-17):** a null `lead_id` does NOT prove a deal is Sales-sourced. The only positive evidence of Sales origin in the current model is the deal's channel resolving to the explicit **"Sales Generated"** top-level channel. Breakdown of the 69 leadless HPP rows by top-level channel (aggregate, taxonomy labels only):

| Top-level channel | HPP rows | Classified as |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 - Sales Generated | 27 | Sales-sourced |
| 2026 - Sales Generated | 6 | Sales-sourced |
| 2025 - Website | 13 | No linked lead (neutral) |
| 2026 - Website | 12 | No linked lead (neutral) |
| 2025 - Events | 6 | No linked lead (neutral) |
| 2025 - Marketing SDR | 2 | No linked lead (neutral) |
| 2026 - Marketing SDR | 1 | No linked lead (neutral) |
| 2026 - Content Syndication | 1 | No linked lead (neutral) |
| 2026 - Events | 1 | No linked lead (neutral) |

So **33 of 69** leadless HPP deals are provably Sales-sourced; the other **36** are on Marketing channels and must NOT be labelled Sales-sourced.

**Disposition:** the Sankey routes a leadless deal through `Sales-sourced -> HPP` only when its channel is "Sales Generated", and through the neutral `No linked lead -> HPP` otherwise. The earlier framing ("every deal is Sales-sourced") was an overclaim and is withdrawn.
